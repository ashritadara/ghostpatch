# Fictional eligibility policy v1
This is an independent developer-approved synthetic fixture, not real insurance or medical policy.
AGE_ELIGIBILITY: Applicants aged 65 or older are eligible; age 65 is included.
DUPLICATES: A submission explicitly marked duplicate is rejected with zero payable amount.
ROUNDING: Eligible amounts use decimal arithmetic, rounded to cents using ROUND_HALF_UP.
REVIEW: An eligible amount greater than 500 requires review; 500 exactly is approved.
INVALID_INPUT: Negative/noninteger ages and negative or invalid amounts raise ValueError.
No patient records, identifiers, insurer requirements, or regulatory claims are represented.
POLICY_V1_1: An optional future fictional policy includes age 66 and older; activate only with explicit human approval.
